## Key decisions (from the PRD, pending Ben's review)
- **Scoring:** exact score = 5 pts, correct result (H/D/A) = 2 pts, otherwise 0. Points don't stack (exact = 5, not 7).
- **Scoring edge cases:** no pick = 0; full-time score (90 + stoppage) counts; postponed games carry picks over; corrected results recalculate.
- **Tie-breaks:** most exact scores, then most correct results, then shared position.
- **Picks lock** per match at its own kick-off; everyone's picks become visible after kick-off.
- **Scope v1:** one private group, invite link, Premier League only, mobile-friendly website (no app), no betting/money.
- **Theme:** Premier League colours (purple #37003C, pink #E90052, green #00FF85, cyan #04F5FF, white — approximate). Use colours only, not the PL logo/name, to avoid trademark issues.
- **Proposed stack:** Next.js on Vercel + Supabase (sign-in + database) + football-data.org for fixtures/scores, all free tiers. A sync job runs every ~15 min on matchdays.
